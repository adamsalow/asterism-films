Philosophy

Every community has its own constellation.

Every family. Every school. Every business. Every town. Every life.

The stories are already there. Most people simply never stop long enough to notice them.

We pay attention. We find the connections. We discover the pattern.

About

An independent studio built around attention.

Asterism Films is an independent film studio based in Middle Tennessee, creating documentaries, narrative films, and thoughtful commercial stories.

Founded by Adam Salow, the studio begins with curiosity: looking carefully, listening patiently, and finding the human pattern inside every story.
